# Data — Dravet-Syndrome-Deep-Learning-Work

## Summary
The EEG data (EDF files) used in this study contain sensitive patient information and **cannot** be published in a public repository. The analysis code and trained models are fully available in this repository to ensure reproducibility. Data access is granted on a case-by-case basis in accordance with institutional and ethical rules.

## What we ask of requesters
To protect patient privacy and comply with ethics requirements, requesters must:

- Provide an institutional/university affiliation and a valid institutional email.
- Describe intended use and confirm the research is for bona fide non-commercial research or teaching.
- Provide IRB/ethics approval information OR a valid justification and local supervisor approval if IRB is not required in your jurisdiction.
- Sign a Data Use Agreement (DUA) / agree to the repo's confidentiality conditions via the Google Form.

## Data sharing workflow
1. Requestor submits the Google Form.
2. The corresponding author (or data custodian) reviews the request.
3. If approved, the requestor must accept or sign the Data Use Agreement.
4. Data will be shared securely (e.g., private Google Drive / institutional transfer) with access limited to approved people for a specified period.
5. Re-sharing of data is prohibited.
